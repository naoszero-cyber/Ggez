# NEON REQUIEM (vertical slice)
Top-down neo-noir action shooter, HTML5 Canvas + vanilla JS, zero dependencies, all art/audio procedural.

**Run:** open `index.html` in a browser (plain scripts, no server needed). Optional: `python3 -m http.server`.

**Desktop:** WASD move · mouse aim · LMB fire · RMB precise aim · Space melee · Shift dodge · R reload · Q swap weapon · E use (doors, pick up weapons) · Esc pause. All keys except Esc can be changed in Settings > Key bindings.
**Sandevistan (F):** toggle on/off, no cooldown and no time limit. Slow-mo (everything but you at 5% speed), Edgerunners-style effect (`js/sandfx.js`): a short activation burst (RGB glitch, white and green flash), a one-hue screen tint that ages with the gauge (teal, green, olive, orange, red) with you and the colour-aged afterimage chain drawn on top, and when you switch it off a blue replay of the run you just made, played back at 20x speed (your run time times SAND.slow, so a 10 s run replays in half a second) as a blue streak with speed lines and blue afterimages. +50% move speed while active.
**Cyberpsycho:** the gauge under SANDE fills over 10 s of Sandevistan use and drains over 15 s when it is off. When full, Sandevistan is forced off and you go psycho until the gauge empties: no control, you stagger after the nearest enemy, shoot and swing at it, and bleed 2 HP/s only while the ghost copies (shadows) are visible. Tunables are in `CY` (`js/game.js`).
**Mobile:** left half = move stick, right half = aim stick (auto-fire when pushed far) + buttons on the right edge.

**Included:** 5 missions, 4 guns (pistol, SMG, shotgun, assault rifle) + melee, Rusher and Elite enemies, doors, crates/barrels, intel, guard/heavy/marksman AI (patrol/alert/chase/attack/search, LOS, hearing, alert sharing), combo scoring and ranks, menus, settings, localStorage saves.
**Accessibility/effects:** Reduce flashing and Reduce motion switches (Reduce motion starts on only if the system asks for reduced motion), chromatic aberration on damage and nearby blasts with an intensity slider, steady red screen-edge cue when flashing is reduced.
**Enemy navigation:** A* pathfinding (`js/nav.js`) routes enemies around walls, furniture and crates and through doors, which they open on the way; a stuck watchdog repaths and gives up instead of pushing at a wall; gunners reposition when low cover blocks their line of fire and flip strafe direction when blocked.
**Not yet done:** environmental weapons, rebinding Esc or the mouse buttons. Extraction is still automatic.
**Tested:** scripted Chromium runs (desktop, landscape phone and portrait phone emulation) covering settings, key rebinding, pickups, HUD layout, and all five missions loading and running. Feel and performance on real devices are not measured.
**Cyberpsycho screen glitch:** `cyGlitch()` in `js/renderer.js`: sliced/shifted bands, cyan/magenta/red tinted bands and scanlines with a rolling bright bar. Strong while the ghosts are visible, faint and intermittent when they are gone. Off with Reduce motion; half strength with Reduce flashing.
**Gunshot hearing:** patrolling enemies in the same room as the shooter (area enclosed by walls and doors) hear gunshots at 2x range (`ROOM_HEAR` in `js/enemy-ai.js`, rooms from `roomMap` in `js/missions.js`). Other noises and other rooms are unchanged.

**Sound:** gunshots (pistol, SMG, rifle, shotgun, enemy fire), reload, explosions, Sandevistan on/off and Cyberpsycho start/end and ghost/glitch bursts are embedded samples (`js/sfx-data.js`, mono mp3 base64, works from file://) mapped in `SFX_MAP` at the top of `js/audio.js`; all other sounds and the music stay procedural synth. Credits: Snake's Authentic Gun Sounds, JDSherbert Pixel Explosions SFX Pack, Glitch Noises / Misc Noises (VZ_GN).

**Cyberpsycho hallucinations:** each glitch burst spawns 3 fake enemies (`js/phantoms.js`, `PH` tunables at the top) that look like real enemies plus glitch slices and walk toward the player. They are not in the enemy list: no AI, score, kills, alerts or damage to the player. Shots and melee dissolve them, they push the player like a body, non-melee ones fire cosmetic tracers with a gunshot sound (no hit, no damage), and they fade when the burst or the psycho state ends.

**Merged build:** combines the `16-weapons` and `weapons-upgrades` versions. `16-weapons` is the base (credits, skill tree, loadout); the weapons and per-weapon upgrades of `weapons-upgrades` were added on top.

**Weapons (floor pickups, E to take; also selectable as a mission loadout once unlocked):** definitions in `js/weapons.js` and `js/weapons-new.js`, pickup letters are listed in the legend at the top of `js/levels.js`.
- From 16-weapons: Revolver (U), Sniper Rifle (Y, pierces up to 3 enemies, -30% damage per enemy), Katana (L, cannot be thrown), Grenades (F, thrown, 1 s fuse), Rocket Launcher (J). Where both builds had a weapon with the same name (sniper, katana) the 16-weapons values are used.
- From weapons-upgrades: Heavy MG (h, `mv` .8 = slower walk), Railgun (r, `pr` 99 + `pf` 1 = goes through every enemy in line with no damage loss, shields still stop it), Grenade Launcher (g, `rk` = the shell explodes where it stops, 40 direct damage plus the blast, can hurt you), Sledgehammer (b, melee, can be thrown). Their pickups are in the missions where weapons-upgrades placed them.
- Loadout unlocks (`LOADOUT` in `js/skilltree.js`, mission that must be cleared first): Revolver 1, Sniper 2, Sledgehammer 2, Heavy MG 2, Katana 3, Grenades 3, Railgun 3, Grenade Launcher 3, Rocket Launcher 4.

**Credits and upgrades:** a cleared mission gives its score as credits (full the first time, 30% on repeats). Everything is bought in the SKILL TREE screen (main menu, mission briefing, results); the separate WEAPON UPGRADES screen no longer exists.
- Skill tree (`js/skilltree.js`, `SKILLS`): BODY, COMBAT (spread, reserve ammo, melee noise), SHORT GUNS, LONG GUNS, HEAVY GUNS, MELEE, EXPLOSIVES, GHOST SKILL, DIO SKILL, saved in `Save.d.sk`. A tier III node needs ONE of the two tier II nodes (`any:1`), so tier II is a choice. The graph lays arms out by prerequisite depth, so a branch can have a tier IV (EXPLOSIVES does).
- Weapon group nodes (`WGRP` and `grpApply()` in `js/skilltree.js`; the upgrades are per group, not per weapon):
  - SHORT GUNS (Pistol, Revolver, SMG), LONG GUNS (Shotgun, Assault Rifle, Sniper), HEAVY GUNS (Heavy MG, Railgun): I damage +20% (500), II-a magazine +40% (500), II-b handling = shots 15% faster and reload 20% faster (600), III piercing +1 enemy (900; Heavy 700). Piercing does not change the Railgun, which already passes every enemy.
  - MELEE (Fists, Club, Pipe, Knife, Katana, Hammer, and DIO's thrown Knives): I damage +20% (400), II-a swing cooldown -15% (knives: volley rate) (500), II-b reach +12% (500, not for knives). No tier III.
  - EXPLOSIVES: the old nodes (blast radius, blast damage, bandolier, safe distance) plus tier IV after SAFE DISTANCE: LAUNCHER MAGS (Grenade Launcher magazine +30%, 500) and LAUNCHER LOADER (shots 15% faster, reload 20% faster, 500). Grenades and Rocket Launcher have no stat nodes of their own (they get EXPLOSIVES and COMBAT).
- Buying asks for confirmation first (name, effect, cost, points before and after); nothing is spent until BUY.
- Saves: `Save.d.skv` is now 3. Saves below 2 get every skill tree point refunded once (nodes were re-priced). Saves below 3 have the old per-weapon upgrade levels (`Save.d.sw`) deleted with NO refund; the old field is no longer saved.
- Skill tree values are applied at every mission load by `G.applyUp()` (base values restored from `WEAP0` first, so nothing stacks between runs). Reset progress clears everything.
- Not included: the OPERATOR branch of weapons-upgrades (BODY covers HP, speed, dodge and armor).
